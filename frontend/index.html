    ETD Platform Architecture Guide

    1. The Pakistan Non-Technical Loss Crisis

    Pakistani DISCOs (IESCO, LESCO, MEPCO, PESCO) suffer over ₨200 Billion in annual revenue leakages. Traditional spot-checking hits under 10% because inspections are unguided. ETD replaces blind raids with neural time-series intelligence.

    2. 4-Channel Deep Learning Classifier

    A single raw time series is easily fooled by seasonal load shifts. ETD stacks:
    • Channel 1: Min-Max Scaled Raw Consumption
    • Channel 2: Autoencoder Reconstruction Error (captures structural deviations)
    • Channel 3: Masked-Pretext Self-Supervised Embedding (robust representations)
    • Channel 4: Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) Frequency Projection (detects periodic bypasses)

    3. Multi-Agent Domain Verification Layer

    Raw neural models produce false alarms on legitimate solar net-metering and transformer defects. ETD runs 8 domain verification rules before raiding squads are dispatched, suppressing false positives while escalating repeat offenders.

    4. Revenue Loss Calculation

    Daily drop rate is computed across a sliding window against the consumer's pre-theft baseline, multiplied by NEPRA tariff rates to quantify recovered revenue in PKR.
